# Valuation & QVM Ranking Protocol
1. Valuation Models:
   - DCF 5-Year: Normalize free cash flow, apply discount rate (WACC 8-10%), terminal growth (2-3%).
   - P/E Multiple: Peer comparison and historical median.
   - P/BV & ROE: Financial institutions and asset-heavy firms.
   - Dividend Discount Model (DDM): Stable dividend payers.
2. QVM 4-Layer Ranking:
   - Layer 1: 9-Factor Investment Score (Quality, MOS, Moat, Balance Sheet).
   - Layer 2: 7-Factor Timing Score (Trend, RS vs SPY, S/R, Volume, RSI).
   - Layer 3: Business-specific Archetypes (Tech Mega, Bank, Cyclical, etc.).
   - Layer 4: Overlays (Confidence A-D, Catalysts, Red Flags).
