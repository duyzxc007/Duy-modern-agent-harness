# Trading Safety & Governance Protocol
1. Mode is permanently DECISION_SUPPORT_PAPER_ONLY unless explicit two-step human approval is authenticated.
2. Position sizing adheres to max risk percentage per trade (default 1-2% account equity).
3. Hard Stop-Loss and Take-Profit price levels must accompany every trade candidate.
4. Kill switch can be triggered via `STOP_ALL.ps1` or administrative ops endpoints.
